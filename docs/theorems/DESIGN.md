# Theorem atlas

The explorer opens on the main theorems. View all expands supporting results from the main theorem that depends on them. Keep the main theorem positions stable across views. The plot occupies the largest area of the first viewport. The chapter index and inspector share a right column on desktop. On narrow screens they sit below the plot, with a Details link on the selected theorem.

Use a warm paper ground, a nearly white plot, fine gray rules and dark ink in the light theme. The dark theme uses a green-black ground, pale ink and brighter versions of the same method colors. STIX Two Text identifies the atlas, its chapters and its major headings. System sans-serif carries controls and legends. Monospace is reserved for Lean names and compact numeric readouts.

Node color describes the result’s method family. Use burnt orange for classical computing, blue for quantum computing and sage for general theory. Chapter identity comes from position and labels. Direction comes from arrowheads, and a dashed stroke indicates a dependency through a definition. Keep the method color visible on selection. Emphasize the selected theorem and its immediate arrows with a dark outline and increased contrast.
