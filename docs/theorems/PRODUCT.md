# fqchem theorem explorer

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Purpose

Readers of the fqchem Lean library open the explorer to see all theorems and the dependencies between them. The first view shows the entire graph. Selecting a theorem reveals its prerequisites and dependents and links to the Lean declaration.

## Evidence and constraints

The library and the Kitaev integration supply the two compiled graph datasets. Shared declarations appear once. Isolated theorems remain visible, and upstream prerequisites link to their original sources. The explorer supports shareable theorem URLs, desktop and mobile, and offline use from a checkout. Its public home is fqchem.matterlab.ca. The GitHub link displays the repository's star count.
