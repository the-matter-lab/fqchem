## Aaronson & Ambainis (2014) · [The Need for Structure in Quantum Speedups](https://doi.org/10.4086/toc.2014.v010a006)

- **Paper result**. Turturean, Corollary 1.2, proves the Aaronson–Ambainis influence conjecture on the uniform Boolean cube. For a [0,1]-valued polynomial of degree at most d and variance at least ε > 0, some squared-flip influence is at least (ε/d)⁷. [aaronson_ambainis](https://fqchem.matterlab.ca/#AaronsonAmbainisPort.Main/AaronsonAmbainis.aaronson_ambainis)
- **Derived result**. The pinned proof converts Turturean’s Theorem 1.1 to the {0,1} cube and the unnormalized squared flip difference. The bound has coefficient 8√15/(125π⁴) multiplying ε^(5/2)/d⁶. [aaronson_ambainis_explicit](https://fqchem.matterlab.ca/#AaronsonAmbainisPort.Main/AaronsonAmbainis.aaronson_ambainis_explicit)
- **Special case**. Applies the proved Boolean influence bound to the acceptance probability of a T-query phase circuit on independent uniform input bits, assuming positive variance at least ε. [acceptance_influential](https://fqchem.matterlab.ca/#FQChem.Complexity.AaronsonAmbainis/FQChem.Complexity.PhaseQuery.acceptance_influential)
- **Special case**. Applies the explicit squared-flip influence bound with degree 2T to a chosen orbital population after T binary onsite pulses, assuming uniform independent inputs and variance at least ε. [orbitalPopulation_influential](https://fqchem.matterlab.ca/#FQChem.Complexity.AaronsonAmbainis/FQChem.Complexity.PhaseQuery.orbitalPopulation_influential)
- **Supporting lemma**. Computes the uniform mean of a function on one Boolean bit for the two-orbital witness. [mean_oneBit](https://fqchem.matterlab.ca/#FQChem.Complexity.AaronsonAmbainis/FQChem.Complexity.PhaseQuery.mean_oneBit)
- **Derived result**. For the specific one-bit orbital probe, the Bernoulli antibonding readout has variance 1/4 and squared-flip influence 1. [antibondingInfluence](https://fqchem.matterlab.ca/#FQChem.Complexity.AaronsonAmbainis/FQChem.Complexity.PhaseQuery.antibondingInfluence) · [antibondingVariance](https://fqchem.matterlab.ca/#FQChem.Complexity.AaronsonAmbainis/FQChem.Complexity.PhaseQuery.antibondingVariance)

- **Special case**. Supplementary Lemma 2 (arXiv v2, Lemma 3). Atia–Aharonov, arXiv v2, Lemma 3.
