- **Variational Monte Carlo (VMC)**. In a finite configuration basis, the [Born average of the complex local energy](VariationalOptimization/VariationalMonteCarlo.lean#L66) gives the trial expectation, whose real part [bounds the lowest Hermitian eigenvalue](VariationalOptimization/VariationalMonteCarlo.lean#L77) for a normalized trial state. [McMillan (1965)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.138.A442)
  - [Zero variance and nodes](VariationalOptimization/VariationalMonteCarlo.lean#L95). Every eigenstate has zero local-energy variance. The converse holds when Hψ vanishes at the nodes of ψ. [Assaraf & Caffarel (2003)](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0310035)
  - [Metropolis sampling](VariationalOptimization/VariationalMonteCarlo.lean#L186) preserves the Born law for a symmetric stochastic proposal. The [energy gradient](VariationalOptimization/VariationalMonteCarlo.lean#L209) is twice the covariance of local energy and the logarithmic derivative for a real, differentiable trial family with full support. [Metropolis et al. (1953)](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1699114), [Sorella (2005)](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0502553)
- **Tensor networks**. [MPS cut-rank bound](VariationalOptimization/TensorNetworks/MatrixProductStates.lean#L30) and [exact bond-D cut factorization](VariationalOptimization/TensorNetworks/MatrixProductStates.lean#L41) · [One-site DMRG update](VariationalOptimization/TensorNetworks/DMRG.lean#L86). [Vidal (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.91.147902), [White (1992)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.69.2863)
- **Density functionals**. [Conditional continuum Hohenberg–Kohn uniqueness](VariationalOptimization/DensityFunctionals/Continuum.lean#L254) and [Lieb variational principle](VariationalOptimization/DensityFunctionals/Continuum.lean#L273), [3D one-body rigidity](VariationalOptimization/DensityFunctionals/MolecularHohenbergKohn.lean#L66), and [conditional molecular HK](VariationalOptimization/DensityFunctionals/MolecularHohenbergKohn.lean#L84) · [3D Coulomb constrained search on smooth trial states](VariationalOptimization/DensityFunctionals/CoulombIntegrability.lean#L88). [Hohenberg & Kohn (1964)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.136.B864), [Lieb (1983)](https://doi.org/10.1002/qua.560240302), [Levy (1979)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.76.12.6062)
- **Neural wavefunctions**. [Equivariant orbitals give an antisymmetric determinant network](VariationalOptimization/FermionicAnsatz.lean#L54). [Pfau et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.033429)
- **Classical excited states**. [Energy bound after orthogonality to all lower eigenstates](VariationalOptimization/ExcitedStates.lean#L31). [MacDonald (1933)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.43.830)

## [Imaginary-time projection and filtering](ImaginaryTimeProjection)

- [With nonzero initial ground overlap and a spectral gap Δ, imaginary-time evolution suppresses the excited population at rate exp(−2Δτ)](ImaginaryTimeProjection/GroundStateProjection.lean#L106). [The normalized energy converges to the ground energy](ImaginaryTimeProjection/GroundStateProjection.lean#L150). [Anderson (1975)](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.431514), [Motta et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-019-0704-4)
- [Repeated spectral filters suppress the excited-to-ground odds geometrically when squared gains contract relative to the ground-sector gain](ImaginaryTimeProjection/SpectralFiltering.lean#L45). [Lin & Tong (2020)](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2020-11-11-361)
- **Auxiliary-field quantum Monte Carlo (AFQMC)**. [Discrete Hirsch decoupling](ImaginaryTimeProjection/AFQMC.lean#L68) resolves the repulsive finite Hubbard interaction into spin fields. [Gaussian decoupling](ImaginaryTimeProjection/GaussianAuxiliaryField.lean#L46) gives the operator integral for a finite Hermitian channel. [Hirsch (1983)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.28.4059), [Hubbard (1959)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.3.77)
  - At fixed imaginary time, [the exact Hubbard field sum converges to the unsplit propagator](ImaginaryTimeProjection/HubbardProjection.lean#L78) as the time step goes to zero. [Trotter (1959)](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1959-0108732-6)
  - [Free projection](ImaginaryTimeProjection/AFQMC.lean#L139) propagates a determinant as a weighted sum of Slater walkers. Their trial overlaps are [determinants](ImaginaryTimeProjection/AFQMC.lean#L148), and importance-weighted local energies [recover the mixed estimator](ImaginaryTimeProjection/AFQMC.lean#L163) when the relevant overlaps are nonzero. The exact identities retain the complex weights. [Sugiyama & Koonin (1986)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916%2886%2990107-7), [Zhang & Krakauer (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.90.136401)
  - For the phaseless approximation, [the pathwise weight loss from cosine projection](ImaginaryTimeProjection/AFQMC.lean#L187) is at most half the sum of the squared overlap-angle increments, holding the other weight factors fixed. [Zhang & Krakauer (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.90.136401)
- **Full configuration interaction quantum Monte Carlo (FCIQMC)**. [Spawning and death/cloning with signed integer walkers](ImaginaryTimeProjection/FCIQMC.lean#L184) reproduce the fixed-shift, discrete imaginary-time CI update in expectation. [Annihilation](ImaginaryTimeProjection/FCIQMC.lean#L142) preserves the signed population at each determinant. [Booth, Thom & Alavi (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3193710)
- **Diffusion Monte Carlo (DMC)**. The [finite-state Feynman–Kac identity](ImaginaryTimeProjection/DiffusionMonteCarlo.lean#L50) recovers propagation from guided transitions and branching. On a real lattice with a nowhere-zero guide, the [fixed-node ground energy is an upper bound](ImaginaryTimeProjection/DiffusionMonteCarlo.lean#L176), with an explicit [equality condition](ImaginaryTimeProjection/DiffusionMonteCarlo.lean#L186). [Umrigar et al. (1993)](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.465195), [ten Haaf et al. (1995)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.51.13039)
- **Phase-estimation filtering**. For positive starting ground weight, [exact-grid postselection](ImaginaryTimeProjection/PhaseEstimationFilter.lean#L122) prepares the ground sector with unit conditional fidelity when no excited phase aliases to the target. [Finite-resolution fidelity and success bounds](ImaginaryTimeProjection/PhaseEstimationFilter.lean#L137) use the separation of the excited phases from the target. [Abrams & Lloyd (1999)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.5162)

## [Quantum circuit preparation](CircuitPreparation)

- **Controlled rotations**. [Exact pure-state preparation](CircuitPreparation/ControlledRotations.lean#L122) constructs a circuit for any normalized complex n-qubit amplitude vector, including zero-weight branches. The circuit uses [2ⁿ − 1 prefix-controlled SU(2) blocks](CircuitPreparation/ControlledRotations.lean#L113) plus a global phase. [Möttönen et al. (2005)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0407010)
- **Amplitude amplification**. The two reflections give the [Grover success probability](CircuitPreparation/AmplitudeAmplification.lean#L110) sin²((2k+1)θ) after k iterations. For known initial success a = sin²θ > 0, a [choice of k](CircuitPreparation/AmplitudeAmplification.lean#L117) achieves success at least 1 − a with [at most π/(4√a) oracle calls](CircuitPreparation/AmplitudeAmplification.lean#L138). [Brassard et al. (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/305/05215)
- **Slater determinants**. A [finite sequence of number-conserving one- and two-mode orbital gates](CircuitPreparation/SlaterDeterminants.lean#L247) takes an occupation-basis determinant to the Slater state of any target orthonormal frame. The [occupation amplitudes](CircuitPreparation/SlaterDeterminants.lean#L71) are minors of the orbital coefficient matrix. [Jiang et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.9.044036)
